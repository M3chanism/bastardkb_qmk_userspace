**Yes — different misfires, not zero misfires.**

### What improves
- Fast **opposite-hand** caps (`Shift` + other hand) become reliable on rolls.

### New / shifted failure modes

| Situation | What can happen |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Same-hand roll involving T/N (`th`, `nt`, `st`…) | Chordal still forces **tap** — usually good. If Chordal mis-classifies hand, you could get a stray Shift. |
| Intentional **same-hand** Shift+key | Harder/impossible via HRM Shift; use opposite Shift or a dedicated Shift. |
| Overlap while aiming for plain `t`/`n` then another key | If the next key is **opposite hand**, HoOKP can fire **Shift** before you meant a hold — classic “random capital.” |
| Very light/bouncy T or N | More likely to look like “hold + other key” → accidental Shift. |
| Shift + mouse/key on pointer layer | Can engage Shift sooner than before mid-gesture. |

### What usually does *not* get worse
- Other HRMs (Ctrl/Gui/Alt) — still no HoOKP  
- Thumb layers — already had HoOKP  
- Slow deliberate holds — still work via tapping term  

### Net
You trade **missed caps** for occasional **extra caps** on ambiguous opposite-hand overlaps. Most people prefer that; if extras annoy you, next knobs are slightly longer index `TAPPING_TERM`, or drop HoOKP on one side only.
